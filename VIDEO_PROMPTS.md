# QuickLoom Homepage Video Prompts

Generate four separate landscape clips. Use the first clip's final frame as the visual reference for the next clip whenever Gemini allows a reference image. Keep the same people, bedroom, lighting, wardrobe, textile bag, and bedsheet designs across every clip.

## Shared Direction For Every Clip

```text
ROLE:
You are an award-winning Indian advertising filmmaker creating a realistic brand film for QuickLoom, an at-home handloom textile trial service.

VISUAL CONTINUITY:
Show the same relatable Indian couple, approximately 30-40 years old, in the same clean, lived-in middle-class Gurgaon apartment. Use the same warm bedroom, wooden double bed, approximately 8-inch mattress, cream walls, practical furniture, daylight through a real window, and the same QuickLoom delivery associate in every clip. Keep faces, clothing, room layout, product colours, textile prints, bag and time of day consistent across all clips.

STYLE:
Natural Indian home, warm and trustworthy, premium but attainable. Documentary-style realism with gentle cinematic camera movement, accurate skin tones, realistic hands, believable fabric physics and visible textile weave. The home must not look like a luxury hotel, palace, showroom or Western stock-photo set.

TECHNICAL:
10-second video, landscape 16:9, 4K or highest available resolution, 24 fps, natural daylight, stable camera, clean commercial colour grade. No dialogue, captions, subtitles, text, logos, watermark, distorted hands, face changes, extra fingers, floating objects, sudden wardrobe changes or product-pattern changes. Leave safe visual space around the action for website cropping.
```

## Clip 1 - The Products Arrive

Append this scene to the shared direction:

```text
SCENE:
Begin inside the apartment near the entrance. A professional, friendly QuickLoom delivery associate arrives carrying one neat reusable textile bag filled with folded handloom bedsheets. The Indian couple welcomes the associate naturally. Show a brief close-up of several colourful folded textiles visible inside the open bag. End with the associate and couple walking toward the bedroom. No payment and no parcel handover like a courier delivery. The emotion is curiosity and comfort, not exaggerated excitement.
```

## Clip 2 - Try It On The Real Bed

Use Clip 1's final frame as the reference, then append:

```text
SCENE:
Continue with the same people in the same bedroom. The QuickLoom associate and couple open three distinct handloom bedsheet options on their actual bed, one after another. Show a natural medium shot followed by a close-up of a hand feeling the cotton weave and inspecting the print. Preserve each textile's exact colour and pattern between shots. End with two options placed side by side for comparison.
```

## Clip 3 - Compare With The Room

Use Clip 2's final frame as the reference, then append:

```text
SCENE:
Continue in the same bedroom. One bedsheet is now properly spread over the bed while the couple steps back and compares it with their wall colour, furniture and daylight. They exchange a thoughtful, approving look, then briefly compare it with the second option held near the bed. Include a detailed close-up showing true fabric texture, stitching and print. Keep the behaviour subtle and believable. End with the couple clearly choosing one design.
```

## Clip 4 - Keep One, Return The Rest

Use Clip 3's final frame as the reference, then append:

```text
SCENE:
Continue seamlessly. The selected bedsheet remains beautifully arranged on the couple's bed. The unselected textiles are folded neatly back into the same reusable bag by the associate. The customer completes a simple payment on their own phone without showing any readable interface or financial information. The associate leaves with the remaining products while the couple looks satisfied with the chosen bedsheet in their real room. End on a clean wide shot of the finished bedroom and selected product.
```

## Export Instructions

- Download the highest-resolution MP4 for every clip.
- Do not add music, narration, logos or text inside Gemini.
- Name files `quickloom-01-arrival.mp4` through `quickloom-04-keep-return.mp4`.
- The website montage will use roughly 2-3 seconds from each clip, producing a 10-12 second silent looping hero video.
